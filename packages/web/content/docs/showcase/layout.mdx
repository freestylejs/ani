---
title: Layout Showcase
description: Six live layout demos built on @freestylejs/ani-react/layout - anchors, shared elements, presence and a gallery.
---

Each demo below is plain React with the public `@freestylejs/ani-react/layout` API: elements
marked with `useLayout`, `LayoutGroup`, and `AnimatePresence` with `usePresence`. There are no
capture or play calls. Interrupt any of them mid-flight: every element continues from where it
is drawn. The [Layout guide](/en/docs/guides/layout) explains the API.

- **E1 Layout Anchor.** A parent grows while its child, anchored with
  `layoutAnchor: { x: 0.5, y: 0.5 }`, stays centred on the parent's drawn centre on every frame.
  The child keeps its size until its own 0.8 second delay has passed.
- **E2 Create Button.** A compact button opens into a creation menu. The surface, label and
  icon share `layoutId`s with the menu, so the two crossfade as one element, and the label stays
  sharp with `layout: 'position'`. Close and reopen it mid-flight.
- **E3 iOS Folder.** Four tiles move from a small folder icon into the open folder through shared
  `layoutId`s, while the other items enter from the folder's position at a fifth of their size,
  one after another.
- **E4 App Store.** A product card opens into a detail view through three nested ids: the
  surface, the image and the title. The title keeps its text size (`'position'`) and the corners
  keep their radius (`radius: true`).
- **E5 Notifications.** A toast stack where `useLayout` and `usePresence` share each element.
  New toasts enter while the others slide; a removed toast exits in place under
  `mode="popLayout"` while its siblings close the gap from the first frame.
- **Gallery.** Sixteen tiles switch between a grid and a list, and any tile opens a detail view
  that shares its `layoutId`.
