## Prerequisite 2 — essential purpose

"Snap, never wrong motion." Every visible frame is either the exact specified motion or a defined,
reported degradation. An aged capture is a legitimate outcome; the engine must snap and warn rather
than draw a plausible lie. A test that fails because the correct degradation happened is a test
defect. The fix must make the row distinguish the two outcomes instead of hiding either one.
