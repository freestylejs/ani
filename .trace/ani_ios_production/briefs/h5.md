## Prerequisite 2 — essential purpose

"Snap, never wrong motion." Every visible frame is either the exact specified motion or a
defined, reported degradation. For this slice:
- B is always a measurement of what was presented, never an analytic box;
- an aged capture never supplies B;
- a kept track is translated only when translation is provably exact, otherwise it retargets from
  the measured `(B, v)`;
- every node that may have jumped in a capture-less or aged flush is reported.

## Prerequisite 3 — task boundary

### Thesis: what you build (in `scope.ts`, `plan.ts`, `dom.ts`, `geometry.ts`, `types.ts`)

1. **Capture (§4.1).**
   - **Reads.** `capture()` reads every connected participant, driven ones included, in one batch.
     Each participant gets one `getBoundingClientRect()` (the raw view-space rect) and one
     `offsetWidth`/`offsetHeight` pair, plus the radius read of item 3, which runs after every rect.
   - **Driven nodes.** For each driven node, also record:
     - its analytic velocity at the capture instant `t_k` (the instant its last written pose
       describes; derive it as `flush()` derives `velocity` today);
     - its last written pose;
     - the frozen geometry needed for `L_k`.
   - **Support check at capture.** Run `supportCheck` on the captured rects and offsets.
     - Reading an inner scale means reading the participant's computed `translate`, `scale`,
       `rotate` and `transform`, counted in `stats.styles`. Reading an exact size (a degenerate
       axis, §4.2) is counted in `stats.styles` too.
     - `'unsupported'` is a rotation, skew or 3D component. Reuse `measure()`'s classifier; do not
       write a second one.
     - Store the result with the capture.
   - **Task stamp.** Keep today's reuse-in-task, re-read-in-later-task rule.
   - **Frame marker.**
     - Arm a one-shot marker per capture: `clock.read(fn)` and the owner window's
       `requestAnimationFrame`, whichever runs first.
     - The marker reads and writes nothing. The other half is cancelled: `cancelAnimationFrame`,
       and a token makes the clock half a no-op.
     - If it fires before `flush()`, the capture is aged.
     - A refresh re-arms the marker; `flush()` disarms it.
   - **In-frame bound.** `capture()` or `update()` while `clock.inFrame` takes the capture aged at
     once, with a development warning (once per scope) that names the fix.
2. **View space (§3.2).**
   - Every box (B, A, tracks, retained boxes, the viewport for culling) is in the flush's own view
     space: raw rects, no page scroll.
   - Delete `pageRect`, `pageScroll`, `scrollOffsets`, `scrolledSince` and `Capture.scroll`, and
     the scroll-`'stale'` path (`PlanInput.stale`, its snap, `Plan.status: 'stale'`). Hard
     migration: no bridge, no flag, no dead export.
   - **Reconcile two interim pieces with this removal.**
     - The status condition in `flush()`, `result.status !== 'run' && (result.status !== 'stale'
       || snapped.length > 0)`, exists only because of the scroll-stale path. Replace it with the
       §9.3 rule.
     - The loop after `for (const [id, lead] of result.leads)` keeps lead bookkeeping for ids that
       the plan did not resolve. Keep it only if a case without the stale path still needs it, and
       prove that case with a unit test. Otherwise delete it.
   - `viewportBox` becomes the layout viewport in view space.
   - Update the `geometry.ts` header and every comment that says "page".
3. **B and v (§4.2, §4.3, §5.7).**
   - For every participant, B comes from the consumed **fresh** capture's rect, whether driven or
     not, with nothing divided out.
   - `v` is the recorded analytic velocity at `t_k`, carried through any presence layer that
     changed since the track was planned. Keep the moving-map machinery of `reconcileLayers` for
     the rate. B itself is now measured through the current layers, so stop substituting drawn
     boxes.
   - A node with no fresh capture has no B.
4. **Keep test and re-expression (§5.7).**
   - **Keep condition.** A running track is kept only when all three hold:
     - its new A equals `L_k` within `threshold`, judged under the presence matrices the track was
       planned with (those are exactly the matrices `plannedBoxes`/`epochLayers` already track),
       where `L_k` is `presentedLayoutBoxes` over the fresh capture;
     - its size target is unchanged within `threshold`;
     - its options are unchanged (today's rule).
   - **Kept tracks.** Compute δ = A_new − A_old on the origin channels and `ρ` per axis (§5.7),
     then call `keepPath`:
     - `'unchanged'`: nothing moves;
     - `'translate'`: translate the track's B, A, anchor offset target and origin channels by δ.
       Keep its timing, elapsed time, velocity, size channels and the ancestor matrices it was
       planned with;
     - `'retarget'`: plan a new track from the measured `(B, v)` toward A in this flush's space,
       continuous in position and velocity.
   - **Inputs for `keepPath`.** The ancestor pose scale at `t_k` is the pose the engine last wrote
     for that ancestor. Compare each ancestor's inner-map scale now with the one recorded at
     planning.
   - **WAAPI.** The keep test applies within its flat shape, with no velocity (§10).
5. **Capture-less or aged flush (§4.4).**
   - Undriven nodes appear at A.
   - Driven nodes keep their track, translated by δ (whatever `keepPath` says: there is no B to
     retarget from).
   - Reporting uses `staleReported`:
     - `lastKnown` is the aged capture's rect when there is one (it is a measurement, just not
       valid for B), else the node's A at its previous flush (`p.lastBox`), else null;
     - `difference` is `plan.ts` `moved`;
     - `rho` as in item 4;
     - `translationExact` is `keepPath(...) !== 'retarget'`.
   - Reported nodes go into `report.snapped` with reason `'stale'` and to `onSnap`. They print the
     existing dev warning; change its text to say "no valid measurement of what was presented
     (capture-less or aged flush)".
   - Status: `'stale'` only when no track ran, per §9.3.
6. **Support check at flush (§4.2, §4.3).**
   - Replace `sizeMatches`/`within`/`SIZE_TOLERANCE_*` with `supportCheck` over the flush's rects
     and offsets. An unsupported node snaps `'unsupported'` with its participant subtree.
   - Keep the §9.1 computed-value classifier on active nodes.
   - Delete the dead code.
7. **Retained boxes (§5.8).** A removed lead's retained box, radius and velocity are stored in view
   space as measured, and the next flush uses them as they are. Delete the parent-carry
   (`Retained.parent`, the pending carry in `reconcileLayers`).
8. **Stats (§7.1).**
   - `LayoutStats` gains `rects`, `offsets`, `styles` and `holds` (0 until P4); `reads` = rects +
     offsets + styles.
   - Count every read exactly, in capture and in flush. Update `API.md` and the React re-export
     types if they mirror the stats shape.
9. **Frames (§4.5).** Keep today's rules: a track from rest takes `dt = 0` on its first update; a
   continuing track continues from `t_k`.
